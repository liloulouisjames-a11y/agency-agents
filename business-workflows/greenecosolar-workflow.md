# GreenEcoSolar Business Workflow

## Business Overview
- **Industry**: Renewable Energy / Solar Solutions
- **Focus**: Eco-friendly solar products and installation services
- **Markets**: Global with focus on green/sustainable segments

## Daily Operations Workflow

### Morning Briefing (8 AM)
1. **Market Monitoring** - Monitor solar market trends, regulations, energy prices
2. **Lead Pipeline** - Review pipeline status using pipeline skill
3. **Competitor Analysis** - Daily check on competitor pricing and offerings

### Mid-Day Marketing Push (11 AM)
1. **Social Media Content** - Generate 3-5 posts using social-post skill
   - Focus: Sustainability benefits, ROI, environmental impact
2. **Email Campaigns** - Send cold-outbound sequences using cold-outbound-optimizer
3. **Content Calendar** - Schedule weekly content using content-calendar

### Afternoon Optimization (3 PM)
1. **Ad Performance** - Analyze ad-copy performance and A/B test variants
2. **Customer Research** - Use customer-research skill to gather feedback
3. **Metrics Review** - Daily KPI dashboard update

### End of Day (5 PM)
1. **Performance Summary** - metrics-analysis for daily report
2. **Plan Tomorrow** - Schedule next day's content and outreach

## Key Skills Used
- cold-outbound-optimizer
- content-calendar
- social-post
- ad-copy
- expert-panel (for creative review)
- metrics-analysis
- market-research
- customer-research
- autoresearch (for campaign optimization)

## Success Metrics
- Lead generation rate
- Email open/click rates
- Social engagement rate
- Cost per acquisition
- Website traffic
