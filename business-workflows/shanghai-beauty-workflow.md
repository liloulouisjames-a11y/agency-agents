# Shanghai Beauty Business Workflow

## Business Overview
- **Industry**: Beauty & Cosmetics / Wellness
- **Focus**: Premium beauty products and services in Shanghai market
- **Target**: High-end clientele, both local and international

## Daily Operations Workflow

### Morning Briefing (8 AM)
1. **Market Trends** - Monitor Shanghai beauty market trends and seasonal demands
2. **Inventory Status** - Review product availability and bestsellers
3. **Customer Insights** - Analyze customer feedback and preferences

### Mid-Day Marketing Push (11 AM)
1. **Social Media Content** - Generate posts on Xiaohongshu, WeChat, Douyin
   - Focus: Product features, tutorial content, customer testimonials
2. **Influencer Outreach** - Coordinate with beauty influencers using customer-research
3. **Email Newsletter** - Personalized beauty tips and product recommendations

### Afternoon Optimization (3 PM)
1. **Ad Campaign Management** - Optimize Douyin/TikTok ads using ad-copy skill
2. **Customer Engagement** - Community management and customer response strategy
3. **Performance Analytics** - Daily sales and engagement metrics

### End of Day (5 PM)
1. **Sales Summary** - Daily revenue and conversion analysis
2. **Inventory Planning** - Reorder popular items, phase out slow movers

## Key Skills Used
- content-creation
- social-post
- ad-copy
- landing-page-copy (for product pages)
- expert-panel (for product strategy)
- customer-research
- metrics-analysis
- market-research
- content-repurposer (cross-platform content)

## Success Metrics
- Daily sales revenue
- Customer acquisition cost
- Social media engagement rate
- Conversion rate by platform
- Inventory turnover
- Customer repeat purchase rate

## Regional Considerations
- Multi-language support (Chinese/English)
- Local payment methods (WeChat Pay, Alipay)
- Platform optimization for Chinese social media
- Seasonal beauty trends in Shanghai
